Shipping & Refund Policy

At Plantsindo.com, every plant is carefully packed and shipped to ensure it reaches you in the best possible condition. Since plants are live products, timely delivery and collection are essential.

Shipping & Delivery

  • Orders are usually dispatched within 10–15 working days after order confirmation.
  • Once your order is shipped, a tracking ID will be sent via SMS, WhatsApp, or email.

Estimated delivery time:

  • Kerala: 2–3 working days after dispatch.
  • Outside Kerala: 3–5 working days after dispatch.

Delivery timelines may vary due to courier operations, weather conditions, holidays, or other unforeseen circumstances.

Important Delivery Instructions

  • Plants are live products and must be collected from the courier agency immediately after you receive a delivery call or notification.
  • Delayed collection may affect the health of the plants.
  • Plantsindo.com is not responsible for any damage caused due to delayed collection of the parcel from the courier agency.
  • Customers are responsible for tracking their shipment using the tracking ID provided.

Damaged Plant Claims

  • If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong plant:
  • Record a continuous unboxing video starting before opening the parcel and ending after all plants are fully visible.
  • The video must clearly show the package and the condition of the plants.
  • Send the complete unboxing video to WhatsApp: 8848599575 within 6 hours of opening the parcel.
  • Claims received without an unboxing video or after 6 hours will not be accepted.
  • After reviewing the evidence, Plantsindo.com will decide the appropriate resolution.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Orders cannot be cancelled once they are confirmed.
  • No refunds will be provided after an order is placed.
  • If a genuine transit damage or packing issue is verified through the required unboxing video, Plantsindo.com may provide a replacement or another suitable solution at its discretion.
  • The decision of Plantsindo.com regarding replacements or claim resolution shall be final.

Customer Responsibility

By placing an order, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate shipping information.
  • Track your shipment using the tracking ID.
  • Collect the parcel immediately when notified by the courier.
  • Follow the recommended plant care instructions after delivery.

Plants are natural living products. Minor variations in size, colour, shape, or leaf count are normal and are not considered defects.
