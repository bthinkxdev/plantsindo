Our Story

About the Founder

From Government School Teacher to Plant Entrepreneur

Hello, I'm Anwar, from Cherucode, Wandoor, Malappuram District, Kerala.

For over 23 years, I proudly served as a Government School Teacher, dedicating my life to educating and inspiring young minds. Teaching gave me purpose, discipline, and the opportunity to make a difference in people's lives.

Today, I have chosen a new journey—one that has always been close to my heart.

Plants are no longer just my hobby; they are my passion, my profession, and my mission.

At Plantsindo, we believe that every home, office, and workplace deserves the beauty, freshness, and positive energy that plants bring. Our goal is not simply to sell plants, but to help people create healthier, greener, and happier living spaces.

Growing More Than Plants

What started as a passion has now grown into a full-time business with a clear vision for the future.

We are developing over 5 acres of dedicated propagation land, where thousands of healthy, premium-quality plants are carefully nurtured. Every plant is grown with attention, care, and a commitment to quality before it reaches our customers.

Our mission extends beyond plants.

We are proud to provide employment opportunities to more than 20 people, supporting local families while building a sustainable green business. We also dream of transforming our region into one of Kerala's leading hubs for indoor plants and ornamental gardening.

Quality Comes First

At Plantsindo, quality is never compromised.

We carefully select healthy plants, use secure packaging, and ensure that every order reaches customers in the best possible condition. Whether you are a beginner or a passionate plant collector, our aim is to make plant ownership simple, enjoyable, and rewarding.

We believe customers are not just buying a plant—they are investing in a healthier lifestyle, a beautiful home, cleaner air, and a peaceful environment.

Building a Greener India

Along with Plantsindo, I also manage another successful business, applying the same values of honesty, quality, and customer satisfaction. These experiences have strengthened my commitment to building businesses that create value for people and society.

Our long-term vision is to make Plantsindo one of India's most trusted indoor plant brands, while contributing to a greener and more sustainable future.

Our Promise

Every plant we send carries our passion, care, and commitment.

Whether you purchase a single plant or a complete plant collection, our goal is to ensure that you receive healthy plants, exceptional service, and complete satisfaction.

Thank you for being part of our journey. Together, let's make every home greener, every workplace healthier, and every community more beautiful—one plant at a time.

– Anwar

Founder, Plantsindo
Cherucode, Wandoor, Malappuram District, Kerala
